# Roadmap

Each task here is a choice of the owner. A task leaves this list when its
release is out. The reason for each choice is in
[docs/decisions.md](docs/decisions.md).

## Next

- The addon repository: ask all addons at the same time, and merge the answers.
- The home screen, the title page, the streams list, and search.
- The player with VLCKit: subtitles, audio tracks, and the next episode.
- The torrent engine: anacrolix/torrent through gomobile, with a local server.
- The library: continue watching, the watchlist, and watched marks.
- Profiles, settings, and the backup in the form of the Android app.
- Live TV: M3U playlists and XMLTV guides.
- The first release, 0.1.0, with an AltStore source.
